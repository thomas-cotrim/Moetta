MoettáA project by Thomas Cotrim, FEA-USP

A quantitative lab for macroeconomics and financial markets.

Moettá brings the whole path of a time-series analysis, whether an academic study or a market reading, into a single verifiable, re-runnable chain: official data from the Central Bank of Brazil, IBGE and B3, cleaning, diagnostics, estimation and out-of-sample forecast evaluation. An independent project, built during an undergraduate degree in Economics.

Questions Moettá can already investigate

The contribution is methodological: lowering the cost of reaching the first regression without hiding the choices that change the result.

Monetary policy transmission

Replicability

Datasets that carry every transformation and formula in their metadata, rebuilt from source at each data update. Chang and Li (2015) replicated fewer than half of the papers they tested without the authors' help.

metadata · test suite · verification

The software

Windows · PT / EN · no server

A complete econometrics lab that covers the full cycle of a quantitative study — it fetches the data, cleans, derives, estimates, forecasts and evaluates — on your own computer.

01

The engine computes, the interface draws

Computation and interface live apart. Every number comes from auditable routines built on statsmodels, scipy and scikit-learn.

02

Reproducible by design

Transformations and formulas travel in the dataset's metadata. On update, every derived column is recomputed from source.

03

Verified on every change

A test suite checks the engine and records a fingerprint of the code; the app warns you when something has changed.

Assistant

Moettá IA and Moettá Dev

The language model proposes. The engine computes.

A chat panel sits beside any screen. Ask for an extraction, a unit root test or a forecast: the assistant calls the app's own tools, and every call shows up in the chat with its parameters and result.

  • No number is produced by the language model — they all come from the engine.
  • Actions that save, delete or overwrite data ask for confirmation.
  • Only series names, metadata and summaries are sent; the dataset never leaves your computer.
  • Every conversation is logged, with the engine version and the input data.

Working paper

Preliminary draft · November 2026

JELC32 · C53 · C87 · C88 · E32 · E43

Moettá: an integrated and verifiable environment for applied macroeconomic analysis

Thomas Cotrim

The paper presents Moettá, a local desktop application with auditable code that brings together, in a single chain, the extraction of macroeconomic and financial series from public sources, data cleaning, stationarity diagnostics, estimation and out-of-sample forecast evaluation.

Its design starts from three problems documented in the literature: the low replicability of applied research, the cost of data preparation and the gap between in-sample fit and predictive performance. Two extensions are described: a business-cycle analysis module and a natural-language assistant whose architecture prevents any displayed number from coming from the language model.

The empirical results section is in preparation. The full text (in Portuguese) is available on request through the contact section.
